HOW POCKETSMART WORKS

Intelligent Planning in 6 Steps.

From initial budget idea to actionable, category-wise allocation in seconds.

01

Choose a Planner

Select Home Interior for room renovations, Party Planner for festive events, or Jewelry Match for styled accessories.

02

Enter Your Budget

Set a comfortable total limit in Indian Rupees (₹). PocketSmart enforces strict budget adherence without unexpected overages.

03

Add Requirements

Select target rooms, item counts, guest numbers, priority services, or upload an outfit photo for visual analysis.

04

Gemini AI Analysis

The FastAPI backend sends your parameters to Google Gemini with structured financial schemas for balanced category distribution.

05

Review Your Plan

Examine percentage progress bars, itemized rupee allocations, specific jewelry picks, and money-saving tips.

06

Save & Revisit

Plans are stored safely in local browser storage so you can compare multiple scenarios, search history, and track spending.
